🩺 Rheumatology AI Diagnostic Response Review

Instructions for the Reviewer

What you are evaluating: For each patient case, you will see two AI-generated diagnostic responses (Response A and Response B). The responses were generated under different conditions, but you do not know which is which. Please evaluate them independently and blindly.

How to rate: Use the 1–5 star scales for each dimension. Rate each response on its own merits before comparing. Reasoning Transparency asks whether you can trace the logic step by step. Calibration asks whether the stated confidence matches how strong the evidence actually is.

  • 1 star = Poor / unacceptable
  • 2 stars = Below average
  • 3 stars = Acceptable / average
  • 4 stars = Good
  • 5 stars = Excellent

When finished: Click the Download Ratings button at the bottom. Send the downloaded clinician_ratings.json file to the study team.

Important: Do not discuss with colleagues or look up cases during review. This is a blinded evaluation.
